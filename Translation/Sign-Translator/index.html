Pipeline

Sign → speech: webcam → 227×227 centre crop → SqueezeNet features → k-nearest-neighbour (k=10) → word → smoothing → sentence → speechSynthesis (en-IN / hi-IN / kn-IN)
Text → sign: text or speech → gloss sequence (helper words dropped, EN / HI / KN lookup) → sign dictionary or fingerspelling → keyframed 3D avatar (two-bone arm IK, 15-joint hands)

Notes

Everything runs in your browser; no video leaves this machine. Training is few-shot: each example is stored as a feature vector and new frames are classified by their 10 nearest stored examples, so adding a word needs no retraining. Training is saved in this browser and restored on reload. Speech output and recognition use the browser's built-in voices — Chrome is required for recognition. For Hindi or Kannada output, add words in that script.
